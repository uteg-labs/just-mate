Meet for real.

Lonely people don't need another feed. They need a plan, a person who fits, and certainty that someone will be there. JustMate gets two compatible people to the same table: at a small plan the app makes for them, or right now, when they're already out.

3 · Value

From alone on the couch, or alone in a new city, to the same person again.

Capability

The app does the hard part

Plan: a compatible person, an activity you both picked, a public venue halfway, a time you're both free. Now: someone nearby who wants the same thing, right now.

Behaviour

You only say yes

One tap. A plan is on only when both accept, so you never walk into an empty café. In Now, nothing happens unless both open the compass.

Outcome

A real meeting, then a repeat

"Say hi to Ola." First names unlock only after you meet. A friend, a training partner, or a partner; friendship needs about 50 hours together3.

Evidence

How we'll know

Plans that happen, Now pings that end in "We met", repeat meetings with the same person, and an opt-in UCLA-3 loneliness score.

Health · meet

People with stronger social relationships have a 50% higher likelihood of survival4. Every flow ends face to face.

Matching · our own AI model

Not a random stranger. Someone you'll have something to talk about.

JustMate doesn't pair you with whoever happens to be nearby. A model we trained ourselves looks for people who care about the same things, are the kind of person you'd like to meet, and want to meet someone like you.

who I'd like to meetwho they are

who I amwho they'd like to meet

✓ scored both ways, then added up

  1. You, in your own words

    Interests, four short questions and a one-line vibe. Never a photo shown to anyone.

  2. Two portraits in numbers

    Who you are and who you'd like to meet, as two text embeddings our own encoder maps into one compatibility space.

  3. It has to fit both ways

    Only pairs that score high enough in both directions can ever ping. You meet over something you both love, so the first sentence is easy.

5 · Reality

Facts, bets, and open questions.

Known · built and demoed
  • Plans end to end: proposed from real profiles and free times, accepted on two phones, compass, "Say hi to Ola." with distance walked
  • Now: both phones pinged at once, mutual accept, compass · Report blocks, two reports pause
  • Onboarding: LLM questions and vibe line, selfie described then dropped, moderation
  • 18 public venues in Kraków, every sport covered · 144 server tests in CI
Assumed · our bets
  • People accept plans an app makes for them, if the plan is concrete and small
  • A vibe line is enough to want to meet someone
  • People switch on Now when they're out, if a match is a short walk away
  • Venues pay to fill off-peak tables (price assumption: €39 a month)
  • Running cost for one city: our estimate ~€820 a month (hosting, LLM, verification, moderation)
To validate · next
  • Show-up rate for app-made plans, and Now pings that end in "We met"
  • Repeat meetings with the same person within 4 weeks
  • Change in loneliness on UCLA-3, opt-in and anonymous
  • Learned matching: the model scoring Now is trained on synthetic profiles; retrain it on real meetings
Production path: "same again next week?" scheduling (today a button), verification, group plans, push reminders09 / 10
