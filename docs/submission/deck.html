Meet for real.

Lonely people don't need another feed. They need a plan, a person who fits, and certainty that someone will be there. JustMate gets two compatible people to the same table.

Loneliness and inactivity are health risks at population scale.

The friction is not meeting someone online. It is the step out of the door: no reason to go, no one sure to be there, and a fear of being judged.

2 · User

Young adults who are new to a city and have no one to call on a weekday evening.

Tomek, 28 (persona). Moved to Kraków for a job, works from home. Sunday night, he hasn't spoken to anyone all weekend. WHO reports 17–21% of 13–29-year-olds feel lonely1; in the EU, 13% feel lonely most or all of the time2. Swipe apps give him chats that die. Meetup gives him a room of 40 strangers.

Friction 1

Initiative

Organising anything, even choosing between options, feels like too much.

Friction 2

Uncertainty

What if nobody comes? One no-show confirms that nobody wants him.

Friction 3

Judgment

Photo-first apps turn meeting into an audition before anyone says hello.

3 · Value

From a lonely Sunday to the same person next Thursday.

Capability

The app makes the plan

A compatible person, an activity you both picked, a public venue halfway, a time you're both free.

Behaviour

You only say yes

One tap. It is on only when both accept, so you never walk into an empty café.

Outcome

A real meeting, then a repeat

"Say hi to Ola." First names unlock only after you meet. A friend, a training partner, or a partner; friendship needs about 50 hours together3.

Evidence

How we'll know

Plans that happen, repeat meetings with the same person, and an opt-in UCLA-3 loneliness score before and after.

Health · meet

People with stronger social relationships have a 50% higher likelihood of survival4. Every flow ends face to face.

Sport · move

Every meeting starts on foot. 16 of 44 Mate activities are sport or outdoors, each with a public venue in Kraków.

Wellbeing · look up

No feed, no chat, no streaks. No health claims and no mood data; support lines are one tap away in Settings.

4 · Solution

One essential flow: plan, yes, walk, meet.

Profile

Mate or Date, interests, four questions an LLM writes from your picks. It writes your vibe line.

Match

A Siamese model scores Now pairs; plans use an explainable score. Walking distance, age rules.

Plan

Shared activity, an open venue within a 15-min walk of both, a 30-min slot you're both free.

Both in

Accept plan confirms only if they accept too. Both phones update at the same moment.

Walk and meet

15 min before, a compass opens: a bearing, never a position. We met: first names and how far you walked.

Plan for later. Pick a few times and a place yourself. The invitation goes to one compatible person at a time.

Now. Already out? Two compatible people nearby who want the same thing are pinged at once, and the same compass walks them together.

5 · Reality

Facts, bets, and open questions.

Known · built and demoed
  • Plans end to end: proposed from real profiles and free times, accepted on two phones, compass, "Say hi to Ola." with distance walked
  • Now: both phones pinged at once, mutual accept, compass
  • Onboarding: LLM questions and vibe line, selfie described then dropped, moderation
  • 18 public venues in Kraków, every sport covered · 134 server tests in CI
Assumed · our bets
  • People accept plans an app makes for them, if the plan is concrete and small
  • A vibe line is enough to want to meet someone
  • Venues pay to fill off-peak tables (price assumption: €39 a month)
  • Running cost for one city: our estimate ~€820 a month (hosting, LLM, verification, moderation)
To validate · next
  • Show-up rate for app-made plans
  • Repeat meetings with the same person within 4 weeks
  • Change in loneliness on UCLA-3, opt-in and anonymous
  • Learned matching: the model scoring Now is trained on synthetic profiles; retrain it on real meetings
Production path: "same again next week?" scheduling (today a button), report and block, verification, group plans, push reminders09 / 10
