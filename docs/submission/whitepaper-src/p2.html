02 The product

Three promises. Two speeds.

One faceless profile, two ways out of the door. Plan gives you a reason to go, with time to get ready. Now is serendipity for when you are already out. Both end the same way: two people talking face to face, first names unlocked, and one tap for same again next week?

Three promises

Every feature serves one of three promises. The rules behind them — a vibe instead of a photo, no chat, a bearing and never a pin — are enforced by the protocol (ch. 07).

{{i:users}}

We find your people

Not a random stranger: someone you'll have something to talk about. Our own AI model scores who you'd like to meet against who they are, in both directions, after hard gates for mode, shared activity, distance, age and safety. Nobody browses people, and nobody is judged on a photo.

{{i:calendar}}

We make the plan

One small plan nearby: a compatible person, an activity you both picked, an open public venue within a 15-minute walk of both, a time you're both free. You only say yes, and it is on only when they say yes too. No messages needed: the card carries the vibe, the place and the time.

{{i:nav}}

Already out? Meet now

Pick what you're up for. When someone compatible within a 10-minute walk wants the same thing, both phones ping at once. A compass walks you together: a bearing and a distance bucket, never a pin. Places can be pinned; people never are.

Two speeds

{{i:calendar}}
Plan · when you need a reason to go

The app proposes, you only say yes

A plan with a compatible person — board games · Sunday 15:00 · Meeple Café, halfway for you both — with their vibe badge. Tap Accept plan; it's on only when they accept too. Or send your own invitation — you don't pick who. Group plans for 3–4 are production path.

Removes: initiative · the empty table · judgment

{{i:zap}}
Now · when you're already out

Both phones ping at the same moment

Pick what you're up for — a beer, a coffee, a game — and tap Find people. When a compatible person who wants the same thing is within a 10-minute walk (the default), both get a match card at once. If both accept, a hot/cold compass guides them to each other within ten minutes.

Wedge: consented serendipity — real-time, mutual, faceless, on foot

The mental-health line

Not therapy. A reason to go out.

No health claims. Software that treats a condition is a medical device under the EU MDR (2017/745). We never say JustMate treats anything.
No mood or health data. That is special-category data under GDPR Art. 9. We never ask how you feel.
No guilt mechanics. No streaks to lose, no "you haven't gone out in 7 days". Declines are silent.
Signposting. Settings carry "Need to talk to someone?" with local support lines. The app never plays counsellor.
03 Who it's for

The quietly lonely in a city

Primary: 18–35-year-olds who moved for work or study, remote workers, people after a breakup, students who haven't found their people yet. They can walk, and cities give the density we need.

The hero · Tomek, 28

Moved to Kraków four months ago. Works from home. Hasn't said a word out loud all weekend.

Not "depressed" — flat and stuck. Organising anything feels impossible; a big Meetup room feels worse.

Sunday night a plan drops in: board games, Sunday 15:00, Meeple Café, halfway for both — with a badge that reads "will lose at chess — will demand a rematch". He taps Accept plan: "you're both in". On the day, the compass opens 15 minutes before, then someone at the table with a chessboard. "Same again next week?" Both tap it. A month later it's his Thursday.
