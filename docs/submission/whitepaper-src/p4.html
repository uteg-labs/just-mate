06 Experience & design

Not a profile-picture app

The whole funnel says it before you ever see the map. A two-minute onboarding turns your interests and a few answers into a vibe on a lanyard badge — the only thing anyone else ever sees.

Onboarding in about two minutes

  1. Mode first. Date ("Someone to fall for, a few streets away") or Mate ("People to grab a beer or a game with, right now"). Switchable on the map any time.
  2. First name and interests. The name stays hidden until you've met. At least three interests; each pick opens three related ones.
  3. Four live questions. An LLM writes each question from your previous answers, with four tap-able options — a conversation, not a form.
  4. Your badge. One wry line in two lowercase clauses — "plans the trip — forgets the charger". Reroll until it fits, then Keep this vibe.
  5. Who you're after. Date: who, an age range, what you're looking for. Mate: anyone or same gender, group size, energy, and when you're usually around. "Used for matching only. Nobody sees your settings."
  6. Taste (Date). Swipe six generated sample faces — never real users. Only the traits they share are kept, as one line of words.
  7. One selfie. Described once (hair, face shape) for matching, then dropped — never stored, never shown. Date adds the blocking "I'm 18 or older" check.
What a match receives — the whole payload
{ vibe: "techno on fridays — crosswords on sundays",
  interests: ["concerts","pub quiz","coding"],
  badgeSeed: 2915,
  tags: { verified: true, adult: true } }

No name, no answers, no score, no position.

Design principles

Layout · Bolt

One surface over the map

The map is full-screen; a single surface morphs between shapes — auth, onboarding, select, search, plans, day-of, match, compass, post-meet, settings. One primary action at a time.

Feel · Apple fluid interfaces

Instant, interruptible, tactile

Press feedback without latency, interruptible springs, translucent materials. Haptics are a language: [200,100,200] when a match lands, escalating pulses as the compass warms.

Tone · light, ink, night

Paper by day, night for the walk

A pale greyscale map with warm amber glow, an ink match card, a night compass. Red is reserved for Vanish. English, Polish and Slovak; reduce-motion, haptics and sound switches.

07 Safety & privacy by design

Safety is the architecture,
not a feature

The jury's first question about any proximity app is "isn't this a stalker tool?" JustMate is built so the answer is no, by construction — and for women in particular, three layers sit on top: prove you're real, meet in public, remove someone from your world in one tap. Every safety feature is free, forever.

{{i:users}}

Mutual by construction

Both are notified, both must accept before anything unlocks. A dismiss is never forwarded — the other side only sees "offer expired".

{{i:nav}}

A bearing, never a position

The server sends a direction and a distance bucket — never the partner's coordinates. "No pins" is enforced in the protocol, not in the UI.

{{i:pin-off}}

Zones, not pins

The map shows aggregate warmth per zone; zones with fewer than 3 searchers stay dark (k-anonymity), so a glow never points at one person.

{{i:eye-off}}

Invisible by default

Nothing is discoverable until you start a search or join a plan. Search auto-stops after 30 minutes (on by default). Nobody can be searched or browsed — ever.

{{i:clock}}

Ephemeral and time-boxed

45-second offers, 10-minute compass sessions, a 5-minute pair cooldown. Positions live in memory per socket and are dropped when a session ends.

{{i:x}}

Vanish

One tap, no confirmation: the session is destroyed for both, instantly. It looks identical whichever side pressed it.

What we keep, what we hold briefly, what we never touch

Stored · PostgreSQL
  • Account: email and a password hash (Better Auth), sign-in sessions
  • Profile: mode, first name, age, gender, interests, answers, vibe line, preferences, settings
  • Text-only matching lines: appearance traits, taste traits, character — never shown to anyone
Memory only · discarded
  • The current search: mode, category, intents
  • Your last position — only while searching or in a session
  • Offers, compass sessions, cooldowns
  • Plans: who is going, never where they are
Never
  • Any photo of you (the selfie is described once, then dropped)
  • Location history, or logs of positions
  • Messages — there is no chat
  • Mood or health data
  • Anyone's coordinates on your phone

Your data rights are endpoints, not a support ticket: GET /api/account/export returns everything stored about you; DELETE /api/account removes the user, profile, sessions and sign-in methods and closes the live socket.

Threat model

ThreatCountermeasure
Stalker follows a specific personNo identity, no search, no browsing; zones not pins; aggregate glow with k-anonymity; nothing discoverable when search is off; plans are proposed by the app, never browsable by person.
Compass abused to locate someoneUnlocks only after mutual accept; reveals a bearing, never a map position; 10-minute TTL; symmetric — the partner knows you're walking too. M1 meeting point first.
Plan used to reach a specific personNobody picks a person: proposals come from the model, invitations are offered by the app ("you don't pick who"); the other side sees a vibe, a place and a time; a pass is never shown. M1 people who met and didn't both keep in touch aren't grouped again for 30 days.
Harassment · hostile profilesNo chat, so no DM channel. Every saved profile passes OpenAI moderation; a flagged person silently drops out of matches, zones and plans. Report, from the compass or after meeting, ends the session and blocks the pair both ways, for good; the reported side is never told.
Serial abuser cycling matchesTwo reports from different people pause the account from matching and plans at once. M1 At most 5 compass sessions a day and 3 open plans; repeated vanishes flag the account.
Fake or bot profiles · ban evasionSelfie liveness check (simulated in the build); M1 one account per phone number, optional ID check, bans bound to phone and device.
Weaponised false reportsAuto-pause needs two independent reporters. M1 Verified reporters weigh more; a human reviews before any ban; appeal path.
Data breach · subpoenaPositions are never persisted; plans hold who, never where. There is nothing to leak.
MinorsEvery profile states an age (16+). Date requires an adult, checked server-side. In Mate, a non-adult is only ever matched or grouped with another non-adult. M1 age assurance.

Block, report and meeting in public

  • Report = block. From the compass of a match or a plan, or from post-meet for 24 hours after: the session ends (the other side sees an ordinary vanish), and the pair never matches, is never proposed a plan or offered each other's invitations again. Settings shows how many people you've blocked. M0
  • Auto-pause once two different people have reported someone, effective at once. M0 A human review before any ban M1.
  • Block from Recent (last 7 days, badges only) and a reason note read only by reviewers. M1
  • Meeting point first (default in Date): the compass leads both to a public spot, not to each other. Women-only plans and a trusted contact share. M1

In the build: Vanish, Report → block, two-report pause, moderation, age gates and support lines.

For women specifically — today, in the build

She is invisible unless she starts a search or joins a plan. She can dismiss any match invisibly — on the other side it simply "expires". Vanish is one tap and instant. Her first name appears only after she has met someone in person.

GDPR / RODO posture

Location is personal data, so it is processed only inside an explicit, session-scoped consent and never stored. No special-category data. The photo goes to the LLM once and is not stored or logged. Production path: a DPIA before launch; the demo runs on test data only.
