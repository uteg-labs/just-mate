Meet for real.

Lonely people don't need another feed. They need a plan, a person who fits, and certainty that someone will be there. JustMate gets two compatible people to the same table.

Problem

The friction is not meeting someone online. It is the step out of the door: no reason to go, no one sure to be there, and a fear of being judged.

User

Young adults who are new to a city and have no one to call on a weekday evening.

Tomek, 28 (persona). Moved to Kraków for a job and works from home. Sunday night, he hasn't spoken to anyone all weekend. WHO reports 17–21% of 13–29-year-olds feel lonely1.

Friction 1

Initiative

Organising anything, even choosing between options, feels like too much.

Friction 2

Uncertainty

What if nobody comes? One no-show confirms that nobody wants him.

Friction 3

Judgment

Photo-first apps turn meeting into an audition before anyone says hello.

Solution

Plan, yes, walk, meet.

The app does the organising. You only say yes.

  1. Profile

    Mate or Date, your interests, four questions an LLM writes from your picks. Others see the vibe line it writes, never a photo.

  2. Match

    Shared interests and shared intent, within walking distance, with age rules.

  3. Plan

    An activity you both picked, an open public venue within a 15-minute walk of both, a time you're both free.

  4. Both in

    Accept plan confirms only if they accept too. Both phones show "you're both in" at once.

  5. Walk

    15 minutes before the start a compass opens: a bearing and a distance bucket, never a position.

Plan for later. Pick a few times and a place yourself. The invitation goes to one compatible person at a time.

Now. Already out? Two compatible people nearby who want the same thing are pinged at the same moment.

Value

From a lonely Sunday to the same person next Thursday.

Capability

The app makes the plan

A compatible person, a shared activity, a public venue, a time you're both free.

Behaviour

You only say yes

It is on only when both accept, so you never walk into an empty café.

Outcome

A meeting, then a repeat

A friend, a training partner or a partner. Friendship takes about 50 hours together4.

Evidence

How we'll know

Plans that happen, repeat meetings, and an opt-in UCLA-3 loneliness score before and after.

People with stronger social relationships have a 50% higher likelihood of survival3. JustMate is not therapy: it makes no health claims and collects no mood data or location history.

Reality

Facts, bets, and open questions.

Known · built and demoed

  • Plans end to end: proposed from real profiles, accepted on two phones, compass, met
  • Now: both phones pinged at once, mutual accept, compass
  • Safety in the build: mutual accept, bearing only, one-tap Vanish for both, moderation, adults never matched with minors

Assumed · our bets

  • People accept plans an app makes for them if the plan is concrete and small
  • A vibe line is enough to want to meet someone
  • Venues pay to fill off-peak tables (our price assumption: €39 a month)

To validate · next

  • Show-up rate for app-made plans
  • Repeat meetings with the same person within 4 weeks
  • Change in loneliness on UCLA-3, opt-in and anonymous
  • Production path: report and block, verification, names after meeting
Ask

Help us run a 4-week pilot in Kraków: one student community and five partner venues, measuring show-ups, repeat meetings and UCLA-3.
